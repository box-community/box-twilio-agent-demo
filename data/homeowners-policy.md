# Harbor Home HO-3 Demo Policy Summary

This sample policy exists only for the FNOL demo. It is not a coverage determination.

## Dwelling and other structures

We cover direct physical loss to covered property unless the loss is excluded. Sudden and accidental discharge or overflow of water from within a plumbing system is covered. The cost to repair the appliance or plumbing component that failed is not covered.

## Weather

Direct loss caused by windstorm or hail is covered. Interior rain damage is covered only when wind or hail first creates an opening in the building.

## Personal property and theft

Theft of covered personal property is covered. Jewelry, watches, and precious stones are subject to a $1,500 special limit unless separately scheduled.

## Exclusions

We do not cover flood, surface water, earth movement, intentional loss, neglect, wear and tear, deterioration, or repeated seepage or leakage occurring over a period of weeks, months, or years.

## Duties after loss

The insured must protect the property from further damage, make reasonable and necessary temporary repairs, keep repair records, notify police for theft, prepare an inventory of damaged property, and provide requested documentation.

## Deductibles

The standard all-peril deductible is $1,500. The wind/hail deductible is $2,500.
